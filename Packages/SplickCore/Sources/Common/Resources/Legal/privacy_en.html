Splick explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile app and api.splick.app services.

!

We do not sell your data or track you across other apps for advertising (no IDFA/ATT).

1 Information we collect

Account & profile

  • Email and/or phone number
  • Username, display name, avatar
  • Date of birth (optional), preferred language
  • Password (stored hashed — not readable by us)

User content

  • Posts, comments, reactions, messages
  • Photos, media, GIFs via KLIPY
  • Payment profile & bill-split evidence (if you add them)

Automatically collected

  • Server logs (IP, timestamps) for security
  • Crash diagnostics if enabled on your device

2 How we use information

  • Provide and secure your account
  • Operate social, messaging, expense, and notifications
  • Send verification codes when you request them
  • Improve reliability and prevent abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

4 Sharing

We share information only when you direct it (e.g. posts to friends), with contracted service providers, or when required by law.

5 Retention & deletion

We retain data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we anonymize your auth profile. Delete in the app:

Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account

6 Children's privacy

Splick is not directed at children under 13. Contact support@splick.app to request removal.

7 Security & your choices

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, and industry-standard practices. You can update profile settings, manage linked accounts, or deny permissions (some features may not work).
