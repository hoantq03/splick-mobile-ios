# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 1, 2026

Splick ("we", "us") explains here how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Splick mobile app and **api.splick.app** services.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account and profile

- Email address and/or phone number
- Username and display name
- Profile photo (avatar)
- Date of birth (if you provide it)
- Preferred language
- Password (stored hashed; not readable by us)

### Identifiers and device

- User ID and session identifiers
- Device name, device type, and approximate sign-in context you provide

### User content

- Posts, comments, reactions, and messages
- Photos and media you upload
- GIFs selected via Giphy
- Payment profile (bank QR, account details) if you choose to add them
- Payment evidence images submitted for bill splits

### Automatically collected

- Server logs (IP address, timestamps, request metadata) for security and operations
- Crash or error diagnostics if enabled on your device

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use the App Tracking Transparency framework because we do not track you across other companies' apps for advertising.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use data to:

- Provide and secure your account
- Operate social, messaging, expense, and notification features
- Send verification codes (email/SMS) when you request them
- Improve reliability and prevent abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. Third-Party Processors

| Provider | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Google Sign-In | Optional authentication |
| Sign in with Apple | Optional authentication |
| Giphy | GIF search in comments |
| Cloud hosting (e.g., AWS) | API and media storage |

Each processor is used only as needed to deliver the feature you use.

## 4. Sharing

We share information only:

- With other Splick users as you direct (e.g., posts visible to friends)
- With service providers under contract
- When required by law or to protect rights and safety

## 5. Retention and Deletion

We retain data while your account is active. When you **delete your account**, we soft-delete and anonymize your auth profile (email, username, linked sign-in identifiers, password). Some content may remain in anonymized or backup form until purge cycles complete; we are improving cross-service erasure over time.

You can delete your account in the app: **Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account**.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Splick is not directed at children under **13**. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Contact **support@splick.app** to request removal.

## 7. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, and industry-standard practices. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 8. Your Choices

- Update profile and language in Settings
- Manage connected accounts (Google, email, phone)
- Deactivate or delete your account
- Deny camera, microphone, or photo permissions (some features will not work)

## 9. International Users

Data may be processed in countries where our infrastructure operates. By using Splick, you consent to transfer and processing as described here.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the app with an updated date.
